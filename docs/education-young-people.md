# Formal and informal education (teenagers and young adults)

## Formal (schools and colleges)

NICE NG223: whole-school approach, planned curriculum, staff wellbeing, local service directory.

England RSHE includes mental wellbeing. Supported materials: Mentally Healthy Schools (Anna Freud), Place2Be, Mind, YoungMinds, NHS Mental Health Support Teams, Papyrus language guidance used only with pastoral protocols.

Multi-session skills work outperforms one-off assemblies. After a community death, follow local postvention advice.

## Informal

Youth clubs, cadets, Young Farmers, harbour projects, faith groups. Adults should know 111 / 116 123 / HOPELINE247. Talk while doing something. Youth workers are not CAMHS.

Kooth, The Mix, Childline and YoungMinds are common digital spaces. Check local commissioning before promising Kooth.

## Universities and FE

Campus counselling, Nightline, disability teams, a safeguarding lead. Student Minds University Mental Health Charter. 18+ students can also self-refer to NHS talking therapies. Immediate risk: 999 / 111 / campus security, not only an email.

You can ask a GP or tutor for an appointment, a CAMHS/MHST/talking-therapies referral, or exam adjustments without a perfect label.
