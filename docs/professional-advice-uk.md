# Advice commonly given by UK healthcare professionals

Not a personal treatment plan.

## Using the NHS

1. GP for most first presentations (ask for a double slot).
2. Self-refer to NHS talking therapies if 18+ in England: nhs.uk/mental-health.
3. 111 if you cannot wait and are not in immediate danger.
4. 999 / A&E if you have a plan and intent, or cannot keep yourself safe.
5. Plan the CAMHS-to-adult transition; keep a list of medication and previous care.

## Usual first steps

Sleep routine; cut back alcohol and cannabis; movement you will repeat; behavioural activation for depression; a written safety plan if there have been suicidal thoughts. Watchful waiting only for mild depression without active suicidal ideas, with a review date (NICE).

## Therapies often offered

CBT (including some digital CBT for mild presentations in 12–18s), IPT, guided self-help, groups, DBT-informed work after repeated self-harm (NG225), family work in CAMHS.

Medication in under-18s is specialist. Fluoxetine has the clearest paediatric evidence and should not be used without concurrent psychological therapy (NG134).

If the wait is long, use MHSTs, commissioned Kooth, charity counselling, and 116 123. Tell the service if you are getting worse.
