# Devolved nations and other matters

Mental health law, numbers and CAMHS organisation differ across the UK.

- **England:** ICBs, 111 mental health option, talking therapies, 2023–2028 suicide strategy.
- **Scotland:** NHS boards, Breathing Space, Creating Hope Together.
- **Wales:** NHS Wales, CALL, national suicide prevention strategy.
- **Northern Ireland:** HSC Trusts, Lifeline, Protect Life lineage.

Do not assume an English CAMHS leaflet applies in Glasgow or Belfast.

## Waits

Time to a first CAMHS appointment is not time to treatment. While waiting: GP review, school MHST, 111 if worse, charity lines. Deterioration is a reason to re-contact the service.

## Extra barriers

LGBTQ+ young people; care-experienced young people; autistic people (named in the England suicide strategy); racially minoritised communities; students registered with a GP in another town; rural, coastal and island residents.

Debt and housing drive crises — use Citizens Advice, StepChange, Mental Health and Money Advice, RABI and the Fishermen’s Mission alongside the GP.
