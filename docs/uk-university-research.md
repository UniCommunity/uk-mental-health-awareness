# UK university and academic research

- **MRC Adolescent Health Study (UKRI)** — planned longitudinal study of ~100,000 young people aged 8–18, school-recruited, ~10 years of follow-up. Pilot expected from 2026. Methods work at UCL.
- **MYRIAD and school cohorts** (Oxford, Cambridge, UCL and partners) — large adolescent wellbeing datasets, including profile work on 26,000+ pupils aged 11–14.
- **DISCOVER** (KCL, Anna Freud, SLAM, MHSTs) — 16–18 school workshops; NIHR implementation study 2025–2028 across six English regions.
- **ATTUNE** (Oxford Psychiatry with Falmouth; UKRI SPF) — ACEs, arts and digital prevention (~£3.8m).
- NIHR Oxford Health BRC and MH-TRC CYP workstream (Manchester, Glasgow, Cambridge, Nottingham).
- University of Birmingham **COLLAGE** — care-experienced 13–25 help-seeking (with Bristol and Kent).
- University of Cambridge / CPFT — early identification tools (e.g. Artemis-A NIHR health-tech work).
- University of Exeter and Seafarers’ Charity — commercial fishermen’s mental health study.
- NIHR HS&DR — LGBTQ+ early intervention and self-care models.

Prefer NIHR Evidence summaries over press releases. Ask whether a study was a small pilot or a multi-site trial. Implementation research exists because school programmes fail when teams are under-staffed.
