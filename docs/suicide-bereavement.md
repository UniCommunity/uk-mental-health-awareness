# Suicide bereavement (UK)

People bereaved by suicide have higher risks of poor mental health and of suicide. Grief often mixes shock, stigma, the inquest and media. Support should be offered proactively.

The 2023–2028 England strategy makes better bereavement support a national aim. Local provision is still uneven.

Many areas commission a suicide bereavement liaison service after a suspected suicide. Ask public health or SASP.

What helps: naming suicide without flinching; practical inquest help; peer groups; care at anniversaries; honest language for children.

Follow Samaritans media guidelines. In schools, prefer living memorials over glorifying permanent displays.
