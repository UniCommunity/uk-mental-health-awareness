# NHS, NICE and NIHR

## How the system is organised

- **GP / primary care** is usually the first professional contact for young adults.
- **CAMHS / CYPMHS** cover roughly under-18s. Transition to adult services is a known risk period.
- **Mental Health Support Teams (MHSTs)** in many English schools and colleges.
- **NHS talking therapies** (18+, England) often allow self-referral.
- **Crisis care** via 111. NHS England published CYP urgent and emergency mental health implementation guidance in 2024.

The NHS Long Term Plan committed to expanding community mental health support for ages 0–25, including school- and college-based provision.

## Key NICE guidelines

| Code | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| [NG134](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng134) | Depression in children and young people |
| [NG223](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng223) | Social, emotional and mental wellbeing in primary and secondary education |
| [NG225](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng225) | Self-harm: assessment, management and preventing recurrence |
| CG155 | Psychosis and schizophrenia in children and young people |

NG134 stepped care: mild depression in 12–18s may be offered digital CBT, group CBT, group IPT or group NDST after appropriate watchful waiting. Moderate to severe: individual CBT first-line in many cases. Do not use antidepressants alone in CYP; when used, fluoxetine has the best paediatric evidence and must be combined with psychological therapy.

NG223: whole-school approach and evidence-based curriculum content.

NG225: CBT-informed help after self-harm; consider DBT-A for young people with frequent self-harm and emotion dysregulation. Avoid crude low/medium/high risk labels as the whole formulation.

## NIHR

NIHR funds applied research meant to change NHS practice. Themes include digital tools to cut CYP waits (2025 awards), DISCOVER school workshops for 16–18s, COLLAGE (care-experienced help-seeking), LGBTQ+ early intervention evidence, and the Mental Health Translational Research Collaboration CYP workstream (Manchester, Glasgow, Cambridge and partners).

Search [nihr.ac.uk](https://www.nihr.ac.uk) and [evidence.nihr.ac.uk](https://evidence.nihr.ac.uk).

Also: DHSC Suicide prevention strategy for England 2023 to 2028; near to real-time suspected suicide surveillance.
