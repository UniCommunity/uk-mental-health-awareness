# Fishing and coastal communities

Commercial fishing is among the UK’s most hazardous jobs. Irregular income, time at sea, injury, bereavement after accidents, and leaving the industry all affect mental health.

University of Exeter and the Seafarers’ Charity have funded England-wide research on fishermen’s mental health.

## Recreational angling as informal therapy

Not the same as commercial fishing. Used in social prescribing:

- Tackling Minds — NHS angling prescription pathway; King’s Award for Voluntary Service
- iCARP and veteran-focused angling
- Local men’s fishing groups

These reduce isolation. They are not crisis lines.

Put 116 123, 85258 and the Mission number on harbour noticeboards. Time outreach around landings. After a vessel loss, plan port-wide postvention with public health and the Mission. Migrant crew on UK-registered vessels are within published Mission eligibility; language access matters.
