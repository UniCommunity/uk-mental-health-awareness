# Suicide prevention in the United Kingdom

Suicide is a leading cause of death in people under 35 in England and Wales. This page does not describe methods.

**Now:** Samaritans 116 123, Shout 85258, PAPYRUS 0800 068 4141, or 999.

## England (2023–2028)

DHSC strategy aims: reduce the suicide rate; improve support after self-harm; improve support for people bereaved by suicide.

Priority groups include children and young people, middle-aged men, people in contact with mental health services, autistic people, pregnant women and new mothers, and people with severe physical illness. Actions include reducing access to means, primary-care signposting, and near to real-time suspected suicide surveillance (from late 2023).

Almost all English local authorities have a suicide prevention plan.

## Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland

Separate strategies and numbers (Breathing Space, CALL, Lifeline). Use the current national health department page.

## What UK guidance supports

Restricting access to means; follow-up after self-harm (NICE NG225); safety planning rather than a standalone contract; Samaritans media guidelines; training for GPs, teachers, farm and harbour workers, coaches; postvention after a death.

Asking whether someone is thinking about suicide does not plant the idea.

PAPYRUS focuses on people under 35. Farming and fishing need the same principles plus means safety and outreach that travels — see the dedicated pages.
