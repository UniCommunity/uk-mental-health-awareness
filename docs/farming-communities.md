# Farming communities

Farming combines long hours, financial uncertainty, isolation, weather and policy shocks, animal disease, and a culture of getting on with it. Agricultural occupations have a long-recognised suicide risk in the UK. Services work when they understand lambing, harvest and TB testing, and will talk in the kitchen or the yard.

What helps: Young Farmers and mart conversations with trained signposters; MHFA adapted for agriculture; factual means safety (firearms, vet medicines, pesticides); GPs who know FCN/RABI; early debt advice.

Young people on family holdings often fall between school MHSTs and adult services. Name FCN/RABI and PAPYRUS / 111. Isolation after leaving college is a high-risk window.
