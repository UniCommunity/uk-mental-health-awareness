# Sport and physical activity programmes (UK)

Activity helps many people with depression and anxiety. It works best alongside talking therapy or medication when those are indicated.

Mind (with Sport England) publishes safe and effective practice guidance for clubs: https://www.mind.org.uk/about-us/our-policy-work/sport-physical-activity-and-mental-health/

## National

- Mind + Sport England system partnership (since 2014); Get Set to Go peer model
- Sport in Mind — NHS-partnered community and inpatient sport; published wellbeing outcomes
- Mental Health Swims and Alliance members
- Stormbreak — children’s movement and mental health
- Sporting Wellness — free support for representative sportspeople aged 16+
- Social prescribing via GPs and link workers

## Football

Mind and EFL #OnYourSide; club community trusts; Minds United, Coping Through Football, Kick Start FC, Mental Health Football Wales.

## Rugby and other codes

Rugby League Cares Offload (16–24) and Movember Ahead of the Game (11–18); State of Mind Sport; university academy partnerships (e.g. Brave Mind).

## Club standards

Named safeguarding lead; written 111/999 plan; Mental Health First Aid for coaches; supervised lived-experience volunteers; no diagnosis required to join; do not claim to cure depression.
