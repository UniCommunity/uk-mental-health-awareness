# Crisis and helplines (UK)

**If you are unsafe, call 999 or go to A&E.**

## First doors

- **NHS 111** — urgent mental health advice and triage. Ask for the mental health option. Use [111.nhs.uk](https://111.nhs.uk) in England.
- **Samaritans 116 123** — 24/7 listening. Email jo@samaritans.org.
- **Shout** — text SHOUT to 85258.
- Local NHS crisis / home treatment team — named on GP and CAMHS letters.

## Farming and fishing

- Farming Community Network: 03000 111 999
- RABI: 0800 188 4444 (24 hours)
- Fishermen’s Mission freephone: 0800 634 1020
- Text SHOUT to 85258 from the boat or the yard

Do not promise secrecy about an active plan to die. Stay with the person if safe and contact 999 or 111 together.
