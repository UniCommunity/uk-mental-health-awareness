# UK Mental Health Awareness Hub

Practical, UK-focused information for teenagers, young adults, families, educators, and community organisers.

Health is devolved. England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have different NHS numbers, strategies and services. This hub signs to official sources in each nation.

> **If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, get help now.**  
> You do not need to wait until things feel "bad enough."

## Get help immediately (United Kingdom)

| Service | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Emergency** | **999** or A&E if you or someone else is in immediate danger |
| **Urgent mental health (NHS)** | Call **111** and select the mental health option, or use [111.nhs.uk](https://111.nhs.uk) |
| **Samaritans** | Call **116 123** (free, 24/7) or email jo@samaritans.org |
| **Shout** | Text **SHOUT** to **85258** (free, 24/7) |
| **PAPYRUS HOPELINE247** (under 35, or anyone worried about a young person) | Call **0800 068 4141**, text **07860 039967**, [papyrus-uk.org](https://www.papyrus-uk.org) |
| **CALM** (often used by men) | **0800 58 58 58** (17:00–00:00 daily) |
| **Childline** (19 and under) | **0800 1111** |
| **Scotland** | Breathing Space **0800 83 85 87** |
| **Wales** | CALL **0800 132 737** or text **help** to **81066** |
| **Northern Ireland** | Lifeline **0808 808 8000** |

This repository is **education, not NHS treatment**. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace your GP, CAMHS/CYPMHS, or a crisis team.

## Guiding principles

1. Crisis information first. No description of methods of harm.
2. Cite NHS, NICE, NIHR, DHSC, Public Health agencies, and named charities.
3. Asking someone if they are thinking about suicide does not plant the idea.
4. Rural and coastal work cultures need services that come to the yard or the quay.
5. Sport and informal education are adjuncts to care, not replacements when risk is high.
